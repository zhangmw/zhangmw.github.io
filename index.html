Smellycat Backup

Smellycat Backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own network storage device and stores the owner's encrypted backups in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no public service, sign-up or user accounts.

Privacy policy

Smellycat Backup 是仅供本人使用的备份工具，运行在本人的 NAS 上，把本人的加密备份存到本人的 Google Drive，不向他人提供服务。
