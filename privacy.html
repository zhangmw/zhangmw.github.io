Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Smellycat Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner to back up the owner's own data to the owner's own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

  • It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, so it can only see and manage files that it creates itself. It cannot read any other files in Google Drive.
  • It does not access email, contacts, calendars or any other Google data.

How data is handled

  • Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's own device before they are uploaded.
  • No data is collected by the developer, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • The Google authorization is stored only on the owner's own device. It can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the GitHub account that publishes this page: github.com/zhangmw.
